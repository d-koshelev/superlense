# Human-Agent Mutual Understanding — Knowledge Note

## Boundary

Align the literal request, intended outcome, premises, and permissible operator decision before work continues.

This note explains the contract; it is not loaded into the default runtime
context and it does not constitute an independently validated general fact.

## Interpretation

Material conflicts change decisions or outcomes. Minor wording errors do not. A warning becomes an operator override only after the consequence is visible and the option remains permissible.

## Practical signals

- Trigger: Every material request; immediately when a premise or intent mismatch may affect the outcome.
- Expected effect dimensions: Epistemic Discipline, Goal Retention, Context Quality
- Primary aberration risk: overcorrection, argumentativeness, or invented intent

## Examples

- Positive: apply the compact rules only when their inputs and trigger are
  present, then return the required output without naming the Lens to the user.
- Negative: BLIND_AGREEMENT.
- Uncertain: preserve the unknown rather than inventing a premise or status.

## Evidence boundary

The operator-supplied package defines this contract. Local P1 validation can
prove structure, routing, and deterministic fixture behavior; real human and
cross-provider effects remain unavailable.
