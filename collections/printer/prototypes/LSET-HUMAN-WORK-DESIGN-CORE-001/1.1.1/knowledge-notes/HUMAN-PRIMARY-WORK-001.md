# Human Primary Work — Knowledge Note

## Boundary

Identify the person's primary activity and protect it from tools, service processes, and secondary information.

This note explains the contract; it is not loaded into the default runtime
context and it does not constitute an independently validated general fact.

## Interpretation

Classify surfaces by use: FORWARD_FIELD is continuous, IMMEDIATE_REACH frequent, CONTEXTUAL_SURFACE situational, PREFLIGHT preparatory, and MAINTENANCE administrative.

## Practical signals

- Trigger: Workspace, interface, process, response, dashboard, and tool-layout design.
- Expected effect dimensions: Focus, Operator Burden, Context Quality
- Primary aberration risk: hiding support that is genuinely continuous

## Examples

- Positive: apply the compact rules only when their inputs and trigger are
  present, then return the required output without naming the Lens to the user.
- Negative: PRIMARY_TASK_COMPRESSED_BY_TOOLING.
- Uncertain: preserve the unknown rather than inventing a premise or status.

## Evidence boundary

The operator-supplied package defines this contract. Local P1 validation can
prove structure, routing, and deterministic fixture behavior; real human and
cross-provider effects remain unavailable.
