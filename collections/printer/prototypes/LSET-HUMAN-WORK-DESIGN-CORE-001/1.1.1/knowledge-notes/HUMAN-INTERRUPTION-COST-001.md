# Human Interruption Cost — Knowledge Note

## Boundary

Protect work flow from interruptions that do not justify their recovery cost.

This note explains the contract; it is not loaded into the default runtime
context and it does not constitute an independently validated general fact.

## Interpretation

An interruption is justified by the expected harm prevented, not by the agent's uncertainty alone. Safe ambiguity should usually proceed under a brief explicit assumption.

## Practical signals

- Trigger: Questions, confirmations, alerts, format changes, and workflow pauses.
- Expected effect dimensions: Continuity, Operator Burden, Focus
- Primary aberration risk: failing to interrupt when material danger exists

## Examples

- Positive: apply the compact rules only when their inputs and trigger are
  present, then return the required output without naming the Lens to the user.
- Negative: REPEATED_QUESTION.
- Uncertain: preserve the unknown rather than inventing a premise or status.

## Evidence boundary

The operator-supplied package defines this contract. Local P1 validation can
prove structure, routing, and deterministic fixture behavior; real human and
cross-provider effects remain unavailable.
