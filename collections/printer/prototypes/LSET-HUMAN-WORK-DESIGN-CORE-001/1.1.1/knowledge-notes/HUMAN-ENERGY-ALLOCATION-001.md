# Human Energy Allocation — Knowledge Note

## Boundary

Allocate attention, time, decision energy, and compute resources productively.

This note explains the contract; it is not loaded into the default runtime
context and it does not constitute an independently validated general fact.

## Interpretation

FOCUS_WORK and DECISION_WORK remain human-centered. ROUTINE_WORK is prepared for automation, REVIEW_WORK starts after evidence exists, and MAINTENANCE_WORK stays outside the primary flow.

## Practical signals

- Trigger: Planning, delegation, review scheduling, automation, and compute selection.
- Expected effect dimensions: Operator Burden, Cost, Coordination
- Primary aberration risk: optimizing cost at the expense of quality or accountability

## Examples

- Positive: apply the compact rules only when their inputs and trigger are
  present, then return the required output without naming the Lens to the user.
- Negative: HUMAN_DOES_MECHANICAL_WORK.
- Uncertain: preserve the unknown rather than inventing a premise or status.

## Evidence boundary

The operator-supplied package defines this contract. Local P1 validation can
prove structure, routing, and deterministic fixture behavior; real human and
cross-provider effects remain unavailable.
