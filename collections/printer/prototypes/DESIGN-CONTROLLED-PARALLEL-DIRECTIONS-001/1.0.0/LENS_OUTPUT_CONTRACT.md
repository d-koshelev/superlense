# Output contract

- `parallel_architecture_status`
- `candidate_records`
- `shared_invariants`
- `source_of_truth`
- `evaluation_criteria`
- `selection_or_coexistence_condition`
- `merge_or_retirement_condition`
- `operator_override_or_none`

Material errors are reported before strengths. Trivial low-impact decisions do
not trigger an interruption.
