# Output contract

- `complexity_status`
- `real_user_problem`
- `current_evidence`
- `simplest_sufficient_solution`
- `costs`
- `future_value`
- `reversibility`
- `warning_or_none`
- `operator_override_or_none`

Material errors are reported before strengths. Trivial low-impact decisions do
not trigger an interruption.
